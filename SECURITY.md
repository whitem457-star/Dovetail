# Security

Dovetail has no internet access, no accounts and no server, so most security problems in other apps can't happen here. Problems can still exist: a backup file that does something it shouldn't when opened, another app reaching Dovetail's data, or a way round the app lock.

## Reporting a problem

Please email **dovetail.feedback@gmail.com** with "Security" in the subject, instead of opening a public issue. Say what you found, the version (Settings › About), and how to reproduce it if you can. Please don't include anyone's real financial details; a made-up example is enough.

You'll get a reply as soon as possible, normally within a week. A fix will be released before the problem is described publicly.

## What's in scope

- The Android app (the `app/` folder) and the page it shows (the `web/` folder).
- Opening backup files from other apps, the share sheet, the app lock and reminder notifications.

Your phone, Android itself and the apps you share files with are outside Dovetail's control.

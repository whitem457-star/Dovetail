# Dovetail privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026.

Dovetail is a free Android app that works out the money transfers a household makes between its bank accounts each payday. It is made by Marcus White, one person, and it is free software (GNU GPL, version 3 or later).

## The short version

Dovetail collects nothing. It has no internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere. Your figures stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your figures** (people, accounts, bills, budgets, savings, pay, ticks, reminders and settings) are kept inside the app on your phone. No one else can read them, including us.
- **Automatic backups** of those figures are kept in the app's own private storage on your phone.
- **Android's own backup:** if your phone backs up to your Google account, only those backup files are included, and only when your phone can encrypt them end to end with your screen lock. Dovetail never sees or controls that backup; it's part of Android and your Google account.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

Dovetail can't connect to the internet. Things leave your phone only when you tap a button that hands them to another app you pick:

- **Share backup file**, **Share for analysis** and **Share spreadsheet**: the file goes to the app you choose (for example email, messages or Files).
- **Share** on the Pay tab: you choose to send the month's transfers as a message, or an update file to open on another phone (with the transfers as its message), to the app you choose. The update file is a whole backup, so it holds all your figures. With the example household, only the message is sent.
- **Send feedback**: opens your email app with a message to us. It includes the app's version, your phone's Android and WebView versions and screen size, how many people, accounts, bills and months you have, and any recent error messages from the app, but **never your names or amounts**. You see the whole message and can change it before you send it.

What happens to anything you share is up to you and the app you share it with.

## If you email us

If you send feedback or email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, keep it no longer than needed, never share or sell it, and delete it if you ask.

## Permissions

- **Biometrics** (USE_BIOMETRIC): only for the optional app lock. Your fingerprint or face never reaches the app; Android checks it.
- **Notifications** (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only if you switch on reminder notifications. Android asks you first.
- **Run at start-up and keep the phone awake briefly** (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK): so the daily reminder check still runs after your phone restarts.

Dovetail has **no internet permission**, and no access to your contacts, camera, location, files or other apps.

## Google Play

If you install Dovetail from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and crash reports under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That is Google's service, not the app: Dovetail itself sends nothing.

## Children

Dovetail is meant for adults managing household money and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are likely to be small.
